## Description

# Project Ascension (alpha)

A handcrafted campaign built on mods we wrote ourselves. You start on a primitive Earth. Space is reachable only by ship, and
every world past the Moon is a place you choose to go, not a gate you are forced through.

**This is an alpha.** It is released so that people can break it. A lot of it has been checked by machine and not yet played by
anyone but its author. Read "Known issues" before you start, and please report what you find.

## The road

- **Earth.** Survive, build, and learn the book (press **J**). Vanilla's Nether and End portals are dead: those worlds are planets now.
- **Ships.** A Create Aeronautics airship that climbs past the top of the sky is carried into space with its crew. Space has no air:
  carry tanks, or build an Air Compressor and an Air Dispenser (Create powered) and make your own.
- **The Moon.** A pale world with no air, its own weather, an archive of tablets, and a boss at the end of a citadel.
- **The Nether.** The real Nether with its roof taken off. Something in the air harms you: wear gold.
- **The End.** An asteroid belt of floating islands with the Ender Dragon, and the Elytra waiting on the outer islands.
- **Gates.** Beat a world's boss and you can light its gate: instant travel between lit gates, for good.
- **Nothing is required.** Go where you like; worlds reward you, they do not unlock the next one.

## What is in it

- **Our own mods:** the atmosphere (air, tanks, sealed rooms), the planets, a League-style item, stat, rune and growth system with 26
  legendary items, a rebuilt HUD, and compatibility jars that tie the rest together.
- **Create, Create Aeronautics and Sable** for the ships, **Create: Simulated Thrusters** for the rockets.
- **Cataclysm** for the bosses, **Better Combat**, **Enderman Overhaul**.
- **A quest book** (FTB Quests) of thirteen pages that teaches Create, ships and the whole road, and never blocks a path.
- **For groups:** FTB Teams and FTB Chunks, Lootr (everyone gets their own loot), Sophisticated Storage and Backpacks, Tom's Simple
  Storage, and Waystones (fast travel inside a world, never between worlds).
- **Performance:** Sodium, Lithium, FerriteCore, ModernFix, Distant Horizons and friends. Iris is installed; no shader pack is (see known issues).

## Requirements

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.249, Java 21 (the CurseForge app supplies it). Give the game **6 GB or more**, 8 GB if you can; we
have not measured the least that works. **Start a new world:** a world keeps the dimensions it was made with, so an old world will not
have the roofless Nether or the End as a planet.

## Known issues, in short

Shaders are not supported in space yet. Ships holding air are not proven. Two players at once are not proven. The full list, and what
to do about each, is in KNOWN_ISSUES.md.

## Reporting a bug

`crash-reports/`. **Where to send it: TO BE FILLED IN BEFORE PUBLISHING.**
