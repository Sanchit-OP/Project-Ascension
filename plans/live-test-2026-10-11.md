# Live test, 2026-10-11: the first light and the airship road

Dedicated dev server, fresh world `airship-test`, everything built up to the refactor pass
(`0900d40`). Join `localhost`. Claude runs the setup commands over RCON; you look and say what you
see.

No Aeronautics ship exists yet, so the ship is a test ship made by command: a 5×5 plank deck with a
chest of 5 diamonds and an item frame holding a compass. Pushes from Sable's own physics command
stand in for engines. Creative mode for the ship parts: you don't need air in space or on the Moon,
and the ship has no balloons, so it falls hard when it lands.

## 1. The first light (on Earth, at night)

| Step | Expect |
|---|---|
| Night, look at the Moon. No gate lit. | Our Moon, no light on it. |
| Claude lights Earth's gate. | A small light appears on the Moon's face; the mark's first glyph on your hand. |
| Claude lights the Moon's gate, then a third test gate. | The light gets brighter each time. |

## 2. Walking to space is closed (survival)

| Step | Expect |
|---|---|
| Survival, Claude puts you on a glass block at y 319. | "The sky ends here. Only a ship can carry you further." in the action bar. You stay on Earth. |
| Switch to creative while still up there. | You go straight to space (creative is never stopped). |
| Claude brings you home. | Back on Earth. |

## 3. Riding a ship to space (creative)

| Step | Expect |
|---|---|
| Claude builds the test ship under you at y 300 and pushes it up. | You rise with it. Past y 320, you and the ship arrive in space together, still standing on the deck. |
| Look around in space. | The ship hangs still under you (no gravity). Earth behind you. Chest: 5 diamonds. Item frame: compass. |
| Claude sends the ship into the Moon. | You and the ship come down over the Moon, about y 280, and fall to the ground together. |
| On the Moon. | Chest and item frame still there. |
| Claude lifts the ship back up and out, then sends it to Earth. | Space near the Moon, then down over the spot you left Earth from. |

## 4. The Archive's pylon (on the Moon)

| Step | Expect |
|---|---|
| Claude takes you to the Archive at (−160, −256). | The pylon's carved glyphs: one glows per lit gate (three, after step 1). |
| Claude puts a gate out. | One glyph goes dark. |

Anything odd, say so. Claude watches the server log throughout.
