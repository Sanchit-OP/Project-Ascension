# Live test, 2026-10-10: the Archive, the tablets, the mark

Server: `localhost` from the **Ascension Dev** CurseForge instance, Multiplayer. It runs a fresh
throwaway world, `archive-mark-test`, because the Archive only generates in new chunks. Your
`ascension-dev` world is untouched and comes back after the test. You are op and in creative, so
there is no oxygen to worry about on the Moon.

Tick what works, and note anything that looks wrong. Screenshots help most for the mark and the
murals.


## 1. The mark, on Earth (5 minutes)

1. Light Earth's gate: `/ascension worlds gateway light minecraft:overworld`
   - [ ] Empty your hand. The first glyph shows on the back of your right hand, glowing.
   - [ ] F5: it shows on your hand in third person, and follows the arm as you walk and swing.
2. Step it up: `light ascension_worlds:moon`, then `light ascension_worlds:test_3`, `test_4`,
   `test_5`, `test_6`, `test_7`.
   - [ ] One more glyph each time, and the eye opens wider. Seven is the full mark.
   - [ ] Holding an item hides it in first person (vanilla draws no arm then). Still on in F5.
3. Step it down: `unlight ascension_worlds:test_7`, and so on.
   - [ ] It goes back one stage each time. Repeating a command says "already lit / dark".
4. It sleeps in the dark: `/effect give @s minecraft:darkness 20`
   - [ ] The mark stops glowing and looks like ordinary skin until the effect ends.
5. Illagers: `/summon minecraft:vindicator ~ ~ ~4 {NoAI:1b}` and `/summon minecraft:evoker ~2 ~ ~4 {NoAI:1b}`
   - [ ] The full mark sits on their crossed arms, at every stage of yours, even zero.
6. Log out and back in.
   - [ ] Your mark is still at the same stage.


## 2. To the Moon and the Archive

1. `/ascension worlds gateway goto ascension_worlds:moon` puts you at the Moon's gateway ruin.
   - [ ] Earthrise: Earth hangs in the Moon's sky.
2. The Archive is 256 blocks north (x -160, z -256). Look north from the ruin first.
   - [ ] The pylons are visible from the gateway ruin.
   - [ ] The court sits on the ground: no floating edge, no buried paving.
   - [ ] No crater cuts into it. `/locate structure ascension_worlds:large_crater` from the court.
3. Walk down into it, no flying.
   - [ ] Steps, covered stair and the deep passage's two flights walk end to end, no head bumps.
   - [ ] The murals read left to right from inside the hall; the teal glows without flicker.
4. The tablets: eight plinths. Open each one.
   - [ ] Each tablet faces you and reads upright, and opens its own inscription.
   - [ ] They read as the warm voice, and each one ends signed "Tacita".
   - [ ] No see-through gaps beside the plinths or the orrery.
5. The rest of the vault.
   - [ ] The stores room is findable but off the main path; its chests have loot.
   - [ ] From the "Dead Ring" reader's spot, the dead gate is visible through the breach.
6. Mark in a dust storm, while you are there: `/ascension worlds weather force`
   - [ ] The mark stops glowing in the storm, and glows again after it.
