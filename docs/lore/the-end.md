# The Belt

Planet 4, the End, in prose. The outline of 2026-10-20 (ADR-0028) grew into this on 2026-10-21, keeping its beats; the
changes to the outline are listed at the end for Sanchit. Read with `what-the-warden-keeps.md`, `the-nether.md` and
`the-moon.md`. Rules held: it explains vanilla Minecraft, plain names, every word on a tablet may lie and every picture is
true. The places, the pictures and the tablets that carry it are in `docs/technical/end-art-format.md`.

## What the belt really was

It was where the ancients made the gates.

A gate needs something to stand on that does not belong to any world, because a gate that stands on a world folds that
world a little every time it is used. The ancients knew that, because the folding had already started to cost them. So for the
roads of their last age they did not use ground. They took a whole world, a green one, and cut it into pieces, and every piece
became the footing of a gate. The pale stone is what the cutting left: end stone, the world's own bone, the cut faces of
a place that was taken apart on purpose. That is why the islands are flat on the bottom and why there is nothing under them.
Nothing was ever meant to be under them. The belt was a work site, not a country.

The voice's story is the reverse of this. It says the destroyer broke the world. The Sealers cut a picture of the ancients
doing it, with their own hands, and set it up where a visitor cannot miss it.

## What it explains in vanilla Minecraft

**The islands.** The offcuts. They drift because nothing holds them but the fold the gates lean on.

**No floor.** Under the belt there is only the edge of that fold, and below the edge the dark between the worlds. A fold was
never made to let something fall through it, so it lets you down slowly, the way you step off a low stair in the dark. Past
its edge it hands you back to the dark beside the planets, which is where a ship finds you. The reason is the same as the
reason for everything else here: the road was built to carry things *somewhere*, never to drop them. The voice calls the fall a
kindness. It is, in its way. It is also the only place on the belt where it can reach you without a keeper in between.

**The Endermen.** The Observers showing through. They stand thickest here because this is the closest the ancients' road ever
came to where the Observers are: the cutting of the world opened the thinnest place there is. They look away from you because
they are not looking at you but through you, and they hate to be seen looking. Meet their eyes and the thing inside panics.
They carry blocks off and set them down elsewhere because the Observers learned one thing from watching the ancients build,
and it was moving things about without understanding why. A carved pumpkin hides your eyes and calms them, and water hurts
them, for the reasons the main story gives.

**The Ender Dragon.** The last keeper on the belt, and the only one made here. Every other keeper was bred far away, in a
world made for breeding hunters, and sent to its gate in a cage. The Dragon the Sealers cut and woke on the spot, on the
last gate of all, and gave ten lights to feed her.

**The ten crystals.** Relays. They are the same relays the ancients used to power the gates, set in obsidian stacks round the
fight, still carrying the network's last current. They heal the Dragon because the relays still feed whatever stands guard on
the gate, and she is the guard. Destroy them and she stands on her own strength.

**The exit portal.** Not an exit. It is the belt's own gate, the one the Sealers left standing, with the Dragon sitting on it.
While she lives it stays shut, because the Sealers made her the lock. When she falls, the gate wakes and joins the network like
any other gate. It was never cut off from the others. It was only left.

**End cities, ships, shulkers.** The ancients' last outpost, and the way out of it. The towers are the stores they built on the
outer islands. Shulkers are the stores' own locks, the kind that learned to guard themselves and stayed learning, which is why they
are boxes that bite. The ships are the last crews' ships, and the wings in their holds are how a crew crossed from one island
to the next, since a bridge would not hold in the dark between them. The Elytra is not a gift. It is the wings of one of those crews.

**Chorus.** The only living thing the belt has. It grows where the relays leak, and eating it carries you a short way and sets
you down again, because it is a small piece of the fold, and a fold only ever moves a thing and puts it back. The voice
calls it a gift. Every time you use it you ask the fold for something, and you have seen what that costs.

**The sky.** The End's sky is wrong on purpose. The belt was built on the far side of the road, so that nothing from the near
worlds shows in it: no sun of ours, no moon.

## The beats

1. **Arrive among rocks.** From space the belt is a flat ring of rock and dust round a bright core. Down in it the sky is wrong
   and there is nothing under you.
2. **The Ring of Ten.** Ten stumps with a light on each, round a dais, just outside the pillars. Two carved stones stand in it.
   One shows the ancients cutting a world apart and setting a gate under each piece. The first tablet beside it says the
   destroyer broke the belt. The other shows two robed Sealers setting ten lights round a gate, with the keeper they cut standing
   on it. The tablet beside that one says the keeper is the destroyer's beast.
3. **The cages and the empty cradles.** On the outer islands, the Mooring: three cradles where ships lay, a gantry over
   each, a row of cages with their doors open. The tablet says the cages held seed and songbirds. The carving at the end shows
   a line of ships crossing the dark with a cage in each, toward a world drawn as a black disc with teeth.
4. **The Dragon and the lights.** The ten crystals are the lights of the Ring, still burning. The player who breaks them
   learns the tablet's second lie: that breaking them opens the road. It does, and that is what the voice wants. Every lit gate
   breaks a lock.
5. **The gate wakes.** It is listed with the others. The belt was never cut off.
6. **The Quiet Cell, the one true tablet.** Off the road, a closed room, its seven glyphs set into the walls in the order the
   voice's signatures get wrong. The tablet in it is not the voice. It is the Sealers' own record.

## Where the next world begins

The Sealers' record says what the ships carried. When the road was closing, the Sealers sent the rest of what they had made to
guard it away from the belt, to the world where the keepers were bred, because they could not keep them where the Observers could
reach them. That world is Planet 5. The cages on the quay are the proof, and the black disc with teeth in the carving is where
they went. Its predators are what the cages held, grown for generations without anyone to say stop.

## What changed from the outline

- The belt is a **work site** (the gates' footing), where the outline said "a world taken apart on purpose" without a purpose.
- The Dragon is **made here**, the only keeper that was; the outline left that open.
- The ten crystals are **the relays that fed the gates**, where the outline said "the ancients' relays" and stopped.
- **Planet 5** is tied in through the cages and the ships, not through a tablet.
- Eight tablets, not seven, one of them the true record. The outline asked for "seven, in the voice, if wanted".
