# The Belt (outline)

Planet 4, the End, in outline only. Drafted 2026-10-20 (ADR-0028). Prose waits for Sanchit's nod on this
outline, as the lore rules ask. Read with `what-the-warden-keeps.md`, `the-nether.md`, `the-moon.md`.

## What it explains in vanilla Minecraft

- **The islands:** what is left of a world taken apart on purpose. The Observers' own work site.
- **Endermen:** the Observers showing through. In the End they stand in numbers because this is nearest to where
  they are. They look away from you because they are not looking at you but through you.
- **The Ender Dragon:** the last keeper left on the belt, kept alive by the crystals (the ancients' relays).
  The crystals heal her because the relays still feed whatever stands guard.
- **The exit portal:** not an exit. It is the belt's own gate, the one the ancients left standing; the Dragon
  sat on it. Killing her wakes it, so it joins the network as any other gate.
- **End cities, ships, shulkers:** the ancients' last outpost. Shulkers are its locked chests that learned to
  bite. The ship carries the Elytra, the way the outpost's crews crossed the gaps.
- **Chorus:** the belt's one living thing, growing where the relays leak.

## Beats (one small arc)

1. Arrive among rocks; the sky is wrong on purpose.

3. The Dragon and the crystals.
4. The gate wakes. It is listed with the others: the belt was never cut off, only left.
