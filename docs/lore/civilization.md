# Civilization

## Purpose

Defines the ancient builders, living factions, and their relationship to progression.

## Documentation Areas

- Ancient civilization identity
- Cause of collapse or disappearance
- Rift Gate builders
- Surviving remnants, machines, or descendants

## Settled (2026-10-06)

- **One civilisation, many worlds.** The gateway builders spanned every nearby planet; they were
  not native to any one of them. A gateway's body is local stone, but its ring is always the
  same inscribed gatestone, their script, on every world. See `world.md`'s premise.
- **The collapse is the mystery.** Not yet decided, deliberately: the player should not know
  either.
