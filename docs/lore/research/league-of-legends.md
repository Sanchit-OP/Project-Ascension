# League of Legends lore: small nods for Ascension

## The key finding

**Most of the League items we drew from have no in-world lore.** Their names are real-world
tributes:
- Zhonya's Hourglass: a former Rioter.
- Guinsoo's Rageblade: the DotA Allstars creator.
- Sterak's Gage: a YouTuber. Its passive name quotes Carroll's *Jabberwocky*, which is public
  domain.
- Youmuu's Ghostblade: a Touhou character.
- Mercury's Treads: the Roman god.

Guardian Angel, Galeforce, Edge of Night, Black Cleaver, Divine Sunderer, Hextech Rocketbelt,
Unending Despair and the boots carry at most a regional crest. For those, a nod should echo **what
the item does** (stasis, a second life, a spell-shield, wind, execution), not a story. Real lore
exists only behind:
- the Darkin weapons: Aatrox's sword, Varus's bow, and Endless Hunger (Xolaani's bloodletters);
- Viego's blade;
- Darius's axe;
- Spear of Shojin (an Ionian monastery);
- Gargoyle Stoneplate (Galio);
- Jak'Sho.

## The lore behind the items with lore (summarised)

- **Viego's blade:** an heirloom that kept a piece of each king's soul. Viego's grief for his
  poisoned queen, and his use of a sacred spring to bring her back, set off a catastrophe that
  turned a scholars' paradise into a land of the restless dead.
- **Aatrox's sword / Varus's bow / Endless Hunger:** warriors once raised to near-godhood, who
  were maddened by war against an outside void. They were then sealed inside their own weapons;
  whoever wields one is in a parasitic bond with it. Varus's bow was dropped in a well under a
  mountain temple, where it fused two lovers into one body.
- **Darius:** an orphan who rose to be his empire's "hand"; cities surrendered at the sight of
  his banners.
- **Spear of Shojin:** a monastery where a famous monk trained; dragon-themed passives.
- **Gargoyle Stoneplate:** a stone colossus that wakes only near magic.
- **Jak'Sho:** a living carapace, a second skin that judges its wearer.
- **The hourglass, in-world:** the nearest echo is a time-mage who pulled his tower out of time
  with his people frozen inside, the hour his kingdom fell.

## Big themes that resonate with our premise

- **An "Ascension" rite:** a desert empire raised mortals into celestial beings with a sun disc;
  a hijacked ritual destroyed the capital. It shares a word with our project's name, so our
  ascension must stay visually and mechanically different: no golden disc on a desert dais.
- **Weapons holding imprisoned warriors.**
- **A mountain that is a road to the stars,** and star-beings who borrow mortal bodies; sun and
  moon cults, one hunting the other.
- **An outside hunger:** a nothingness beyond the world that leaks in through "thresholds". A
  besieged kingdom opened it on purpose as a weapon, and it ate both armies.
- **A grief-driven catastrophe:** a soul-trapping mist that spreads every year.
- **World-shaping fragments** scattered, fought over, then hidden by a keeper so no one holds them
  all.
- **A forge-god's star-metal bridge** over an abyss; a floating stone fortress that fell and
  became a living rock creature.
- **Rediscovered science-magic** whose core became conscious.

## How the themes map onto ours

| Ours | The echo | How to keep it ours |
|---|---|---|
| Builders who walked between worlds | A civilisation lifted by star-knowledge, then fallen | Engineers, not god-kings; their "ascension" was the network itself |
| Gate keys | Scattered world-shaping fragments, hidden by one keeper | Keys are guardians' hearts; a keeper of our own scattered them |
| "Something walked the road toward us" | An outside hunger let in through a door its victims built | Our door is the gate network itself |
| The keepers (Moon bosses) | Warriors bound into weapons; a statue that wakes near magic | Wardens still following a dead state's last order |
| The Archive | Scholars who guarded dangerous artefacts | Sealed from the inside, the keepers locked in with the danger |
| The forge world | A forge-god's volcano, a star-metal bridge | An empty forge; the Netherite Monstrosity still tends the fire |
| Earth's Deep Dark and the Warden | Something sealed under the world, guarded by former servants | The Warden as a blind jailer that forgot what it guards |

## Small nods (pick from these; all wording ours)

**On our items:**
- **veigos_blade:**
  - Each king who carried it left a little of himself in the steel; the last left everything.
  - Its grip is bound with seamstress's thread.
- **darken_blade:**
  - An Archive fragment says some warriors asked to be *kept* in their weapons rather than die,
    and the next line is scratched out.
  - At night it casts a second, winged shadow.
- **endless_hunger:** two hilt glyphs, an empty bowl and a full one; it is satisfied with neither.
- **heartwood_of_pallas:**
  - Cut from a tree that grew over a sealed well, found open and empty.
  - Two names carved on the limb, worn into one word.
- **darius_axe:** haft notches that, the Archive says, count gates that opened *without* a fight.
- **zonias_hourglass:**
  - Sand from a tower whose keeper stopped the hour its city fell.
  - A Moon room where dust hangs frozen in the air, and falls when the Hourglass is used.
- **steraks_harness:** a *Jabberwocky* phrase (public domain) for its effect name.
- **wraithblade:** forged for a funeral guard; it has attended many funerals since.
- **ragefang:** a maker's mark of a small winged gyro, for a pilot who never landed.
- **spear_of_shojin:** left at a mountain house of devotion, a serpent-dragon carved down the
  haft.
- **divine_sunderer:** its forge-world name translates as "the one that unmakes makers".
- **mortal_reminder:** an arrowhead engraved with a five-part dirge.
- **galeforce:** strung with fibre that grows only in Moon dust storms; it hums before a storm.
- **protean_mask:**
  - Grown, not forged; it remembers previous wearers.
  - Clear a boss without dying while wearing it and it "decides you will do".
- **gargoyle_stoneplate:** carved from a broken Moon statue of the kind that turns its head when a
  gate is active.
- **guardian_wing:** a feather from something that climbed so high it stopped being a bird.
- **rocketbelt:** every serial plate ends in "-01"; no later model exists.
- **collectors_pants:** a skull pocket and a coin pocket, "the only two certain things".
- **unending_despair:** woven inside a mist that keeps whatever dies in it.

**World nods:**
- A sun sigil chiselled over older moon sigils in the Archive: one faction erased by its own
  people.
- A shattered ring of light above a dais, glimpsed in a dust storm.
- A fragment saying a besieged council *opened the way* for the outside thing on purpose.
- The Warden's kind called "the blind who were set to listen".
- A keeper's journal: they gathered the keys so no one would hold them all; each boss key is one
  they lost.
- An empty colossal anvil and a star-metal bridge over lava on the forge world.
- A slow living-rock creature carved with citadel glyphs: it was a floating fortress once.
- On a late planet, a vast celestial creature whose collar is shaped like a gate-key ring.

## What to avoid (Riot-owned)

- **Never in names, lore text or tooltips:**
  - Riot place names: Runeterra, Shurima, Targon, Icathia, Ixtal, Camavor, the Blessed or Shadow
    Isles, Noxus, Demacia, Ionia, Piltover, Zaun, Freljord, Bilgewater, Pallas, Shojin as an
    order, the Howling Abyss.
  - Riot terms: Darkin, Ascended as a proper noun, Aspects, Watchers, the Void as a named realm,
    World Runes, Rune Wars, Ruination, Black Mist, Harrowing, Waters of Life, Sun Disc, Hextech,
    Hexgate.
  - Any champion's name.
  - Riot's item and ability phrases: "World Ender", "Time Stop", "Death and Taxes",
    "Dragonforce", and Jak'Sho's "worthy" line.
- **Story beats too close to copy whole:**
  - the king, the poisoned seamstress queen and the sacred spring;
  - warriors sealed in weapons by star-gods after a civil war;
  - a desert empire with a sun disc, a falcon emperor and a traitor magus;
  - a mountain climb that ends in possession;
  - a mage-kingdom unleashing a void;
  - two lovers fused into one body.

  Echo the *feeling* (grief, hunger, stasis, a door opened on purpose), never the set.
- **For the combat session (its call, not ours):** five item ids are near-spellings of Riot
  names: `veigos_blade` (Viego), `zonias_hourglass` (Zhonya), `darken_blade` (Darkin), plus
  `darius_axe` and `heartwood_of_pallas`. Fine for development; before a public release,
  replacing them with fully original names is the conservative move. (Not legal advice.)
