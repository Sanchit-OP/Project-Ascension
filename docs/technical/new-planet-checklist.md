# Adding a planet: what every world needs

ADR-0004 says a planet is data, and for the *registry entry* that is true. A world a stranger can actually play has
far more around the entry. This list is read off Planet 3 (the Nether, ADR-0017), the most complete example, from its
tracked files on 2026-10-20. Use it as the checklist for Planet 4 and for every planet after, and update it when a
planet teaches us something it missed. Paths are under `modules/` unless they start with `docs/`, `tools/` or `pack/`.

The per-planet design fields (`docs/progression/planets.md`): fantasy, threat, constraint, objective, prize, mastery
loop; and an orbit purpose, a surface survival mechanic, a movement wrinkle, a unique resource, a core structure, a
boss or conquest condition and a permanent travel improvement. **Settle those on paper (section 1) before any file in
sections 2 to 11.**

## 1. Decisions and story (nothing is built before these)

| Need | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| An ADR | `docs/decisions/` (Nether: 0017) | Which world, what its survival rule and boss are, what is rejected. A planet that takes another planet's slot supersedes the older plan in writing. |
| A design note | `docs/technical/worlds-api.md` (section "Planet 3") | What was built and how it was checked, filled in as you go. |
| The lore | `docs/lore/the-<planet>.md` (Nether: `the-nether.md`) | Anchored in vanilla Minecraft (what the mobs and structures are in the story), plain names, an outline before long prose, voice and rules from `what-the-warden-keeps.md`. |
| The per-planet design fields above | the ADR | One line each. |

## 2. The world itself

| Need | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| The planet entry | `worlds/.../data/ascension_worlds/ascension_worlds/planet/<name>.json` | `surface` (the dimension), `space.position` / `body_radius` / `approach_radius`, `order`, `color`, optional `environment` (breathable, drain), `hazard`, `weather`. Positions for Planets 4 to 7 are already laid out in `worlds-api.md` ("Seven planets in one space"). |
| The dimension | Own (Moon): `dimension/`, `dimension_type/`, `worldgen/noise_settings/`. Vanilla override (Nether): `data/minecraft/dimension/the_nether.json`, `data/minecraft/dimension_type/the_nether.json`, `worldgen/noise_settings/nether.json` (generated by `tools/generate-roofless-nether.py`) | A world's dimension settings freeze at creation: **fresh worlds only**. Say so in the ADR. |
| Terrain checked without a client | `docs/technical/dev-environment.md` (`/locate`, `ChunkReport`, forceload) | Heights, fluids, structures located, ground under every structure. |
| Vanilla portals stay dormant | ADR-0014 | Travel is by ship and gate. Check what a *portal block inside* the world does (exit portals, gateways). |

## 4. Ships (the only road)

| Need | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Sable air data | `compat/sable/.../data/ascension_compat_sable/dimension_physics/<dimension>.json` | **Without it a ship cannot climb out**: Sable fades air to zero at a world's top. Earth, Moon, Nether and space have one. |
| Where a ship lands | `SpaceTravel.arrivalAbove` (`worlds-api.md` "Vehicles crossing the line") | 40 blocks below the build height, over the spot the pilot rose from, or over the world's centre. **Check that spot is not void, lava or an arena.** |
| A round trip proved | `dev-environment.md` (the command-built ship) | Space, down, up again, cargo intact, on a dedicated server. |

## 5. Surviving it and moving in it

| Need | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| A survival rule | The planet entry's `environment` (breathable or vacuum) or `hazard` with a `ward` item tag and a message | Nether: wither unless gold or netherite is worn (`#ascension_worlds:wards_off_the_rot`). Vacuum uses the tanks and Oxygen v2. |
| A movement wrinkle | `planets.md` (every mandatory world has one) | Nether: its lava seas and cliffs. |
| The mobs | vanilla's, unless the ADR says otherwise | New mobs need a tier tag so difficulty scaling reaches them (`combat/.../tags/entity_type/tier/`). |

## 6. The gate and its key

| Need | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| The gate ruin | `worlds/.../ascension_worlds/gateway_site/gateway_<planet>_site.json`, `worldgen/structure/gateway_frame_<planet>.json`, `worldgen/structure_set/gateway_frame_<planet>.json`, a biome tag (`nether_gate_biomes`) | Fixed position or a ruin that **seeks its ground** (`gateway-frame-format.md`). Placed after every feature so a lava pool cannot eat the frame. Pre-generated gates cannot be broken. |
| The key | A loot modifier from the boss that drops a key with `gateway_key_for_planet: <dimension>` | Cataclysm boss: `compat/cataclysm/.../loot_modifiers/netherite_monstrosity_key.json` plus a loot table. **A vanilla boss belongs in `ascension-worlds`** (as `warden_tendril.json` does). |
| Milestones | `worlds/.../advancement/gates/<planet>.json`, `advancement/discovery/<planet>.json`, `discovery/<planet>_gate.json`, with matching `ascension_combat/growth_milestone/...` | These feed skill points and rune slots (`growth.md`). `tools/check-data.py` fails on a misspelt condition. |
| The gate screen's name | lang key | The Nether lacked one once. |

## 7. The boss

| Need | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Armor and Magic Resist | `combat/.../data_maps/entity_type/mob_stats.json` | A boss's tier is in `tags/entity_type/tier/boss.json`. |
| Health and damage by difficulty | `combat/.../mob_difficulty/{easy,normal,hard}.json`, `types` (Cataclysm's: `compat/cataclysm/.../mob_difficulty/`) | `health_total` is exact (ADR-0027). Easy is 85% and Hard 125% of Normal by convention. |
| The balance row | `docs/progression/balance.md` section 5, kill times from `tools/time-to-kill.py` | Weapons are tuned against a fixed 1,000 HP boss, so a new boss does not move them. |
| A server check | `combat/.../internal/dev/DamageChecks.java` (health totals) | Proves the totals on a dedicated server. |
| The growth milestone for the kill | `combat/.../advancement/growth/<boss>.json` and `growth_milestone/<boss>.json` | |
| A heart for build paths (if it gives one) | item model and texture, a loot modifier and table (`warden_heart`), recipes in `docs/progression/build-paths.md` | An addition to the build-paths contract: ask Sanchit. |

## 10. The pack around it

| Need | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| The quest book | `pack/quests/*.py`, generated by `tools/generate-quests.py` (the build checks it with `--check`) | A page or section; the book says what and where, never why, and never gates a path (`quests.md`). |
| Compat jars to check | `compat/chunky` (pre-generation of the planet), `compat/distanthorizons` (`DimensionClouds`), `compat/endermanoverhaul` (End Islands endermen), `compat/cataclysm` (which of its structures and bosses are held or allowed here), `compat/jei` | Each may need nothing; say so after looking. |
| Server | `serverpack/README.md` | Pre-generation advice, memory notes. |

## 11. Proof (ADR-0008)

1. Headless: `/locate`, block checks, a dedicated-server check file for the numbers (`*-checks.txt` pattern).
2. **One batched client run** (not a launch per tweak): a checklist covering the sky, the face from space, the ruins, the
   gate, the boss, the survival rule, a ship round trip.
3. A world reload with the planet's state saved.
4. A performance-log entry (`docs/technical/performance-log.md`) for the new dimension, and a refactor pass every third
   increment.

`CLAUDE.md` (current state), `docs/technical/worlds-api.md`, `docs/progression/journey.md`, `balance.md`, `todo.md`,
`docs/technical/architecture.md` if a module changed, the memory notes, and **this file**.

## 13. What Planet 4 taught (2026-10-20)

- **The planet file can break other builds.** Adding `planet/end.json` made the quest book's Planets page (`pack/quests/planets.py`)
  fail until it had a blurb, a title and a known-after line, and the quest art needed a sprite. Add them in the same commit.
- **A planet may need no gate ruin.** If the world already has a portal-shaped feature (the End's exit portal), the gate can be that
  (`EndGate`): record its position with `GatewayNetworkState.activate` and fire `GateProgress.networkChanged`. Check the **shape** of vanilla's
  feature: the End portal is a ring around a bedrock pillar, not a block at the centre (the first version missed it).
- **Where a ship lands is a planet field** (`arrival_point`), not a rule: the default is over the world's centre, which was the Dragon's arena.
- **A world with no floor** can send the player back to space (`falls_to_space`, `VoidFall`); the Slow Falling dose and the handoff depth are in
  that class. Ships are not covered.
- **The boss tier and growth need their own rows:** a `mob_stats` row, three `mob_difficulty` rows, a `growth/<boss>` advancement and
  milestone, `DamageChecks`' boss list, and a line in `balance.md` and `growth.md` (the growth total moves).
- **A vanilla boss may have hidden rules**: the Dragon's body takes a quarter damage, her attacks have no Attack Damage attribute (a damage
  percent does nothing), crystals heal 2 a second. Read the source before setting numbers.
- **Check with a stand-in player**, not by hand: `EndChecks` (`end-checks.txt`) found two bugs a read-through missed. A stand-in's
  connection is not in the server's list, so tick it yourself (`doTick`). Use a new world name per run.
- **Credits and portal trips**: vanilla's End portal plays the credits for a player who has not seen them; marking them seen is the fix,
  and the gate's own trip must clear `portalProcess` before it moves the player.
